Event Intelligence

The data layer behind the programme.

This began as an operational need: ticketing and customer information was fragmented across exports. I built a system that could clean, connect and analyse it instead of treating every show as an isolated spreadsheet.

01Raw exports

Ticketing CSVs and operational records.

02Clean

Standardise fields and remove unusable records.

03Match

Deduplicate customers using email and phone identifiers.

04Connect

Link contacts, shows, categories, attendance and revenue.

05Analyse

Retention, crossover, revenue, trends and outliers.

06Decide

Support targeting, programming, reporting and planning.

What it can answer: who returns, which shows share audiences, which periods and categories drive revenue, when people buy, which shows behave unusually, and how to reach the largest useful audience with less duplication. The platform also supports customer history, statistical views, audience-overlap analysis, optimisation and exportable reporting.

Friends Like These

A live student event supported by a working digital product used across mobile, admin and venue-screen contexts.

A live event system, not just a mock-up.

Friends Like These was not a static concept. I built and deployed a system that supported real event operations: team registration, QR-based sign-up, countdown-driven registration flow, admin controls, scoring, leaderboard updates and public display screens for the room.

The value was in making the event run better. It reduced manual coordination, kept participants informed, gave the room a visible centre of activity and created a smoother bridge between facilitation, competition and audience energy.

About

I work where people, events, systems and data meet.

My background spans psychology, computer science, information systems, live events and community engagement. That combination shapes how I approach work: I pay attention to the person in the room, the system behind the room and the information that can improve what happens next.

At The One Room, that meant moving between artists, agencies, suppliers, ticketing, audience questions, publicity, venue operations and post-event communication. It also meant building relationships with media and finding ways to keep a programme visible beyond its own social channels.

My technology work developed from the same instinct. Friends Like These used a custom digital system to improve a live event, while Event Intelligence grew from the need to make real event and customer data useful for retention, programming, marketing and decision-making.

I am most interested in work where operations, experience, technology, behaviour and strategy overlap — where understanding people is as important as understanding the process or platform supporting them.
